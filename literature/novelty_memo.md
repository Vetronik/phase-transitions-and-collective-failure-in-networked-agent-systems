# Novelty decision after the executed preliminary review

The executed sources establish that social influence can reduce diversity, that network structure can also improve collective estimation, that cascades can produce correlated mistakes, and that statistical-mechanics language has recently been applied to LLM communities. Therefore the paper will not claim novelty for consensus, an Ising-like agent model, or the generic observation that communication can help or hurt.

The surviving contribution is narrower: a controlled binary-agent study that keeps private-signal marginal quality fixed while independently varying shared-input correlation and communication strength, measures terminal severe-failure risk and error covariance, and separates finite-horizon decision behavior from equilibrium criticality. The reduced mechanism batch provides error-vector data for this distinction. The complete-graph benchmark is a validation experiment, not a new phase-transition theorem.

This novelty statement remains conditional on a complete forward/backward citation review before submission. The 2026 Physics of Agents and Interaction Tax papers must be discussed explicitly because they are the closest recent work.
